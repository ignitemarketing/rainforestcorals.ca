Sourcing

Sustainability isn’t a tagline.
It’s the whole reason we opened.

Aquaculture first.

Every coral we can grow, we do. Roughly 80% of what we’ll stock is fragged from our broodstock, not pulled from a reef.

Mariculture before wild.

For corals we can’t aquaculture yet, we work with mariculture programs in Bali, Australia, and the Solomon Islands that pay reef communities for grown-out frags.

Wild only when there’s a story.

Less than 5% of our stock is wild-caught. When it is, we publish the CITES paperwork on the listing — you see where it came from before you buy.
